# LYNVO

> Linking Ideas to Innovation

LYNVO is a digital studio founded by Ivo Pereira in Sawantwadi, Maharashtra, India. The studio builds, redesigns, troubleshoots, secures, and ships digital products for local and global clients.

## What We Build

- Web development with React, Next.js, Node.js, and Laravel
- UI/UX and brand design
- Brand identity and graphics
- Social media management
- SEO and geo optimization
- VAPT and security audits
- Mobile and progressive web applications
- Web3 and blockchain development
- Custom software and workflow automation
- Digital strategy and go-to-market planning
